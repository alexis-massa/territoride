How it works

The rules

  • The world is divided into small hexagonal tiles. Ride through one and it's yours ({{ cell_value }} points).
  • If your ride is a loop (start and end within {{ loop_tolerance_m }}m of each other), you also claim every tile enclosed inside it - not just the ones you rode through.
  • Mountain passes are separate landmarks scattered worldwide. Ride within {{ poi_radius_m }}m of one to claim it - it's worth its altitude in points, so bigger passes are worth more.
  • Whoever rode it most recently owns it - always. There's no permanent ownership, so ride back through contested ground to take it.
  • Everything decays: a tile or pass loses value evenly over {{ decay_days }} days, and after {{ decay_days }} days it's released back to unclaimed automatically. Ride it again to keep it.
  • Your score is the sum of everything you currently hold, decay included - check the leaderboard anytime.

Is it safe to connect Strava?

  • Login goes through Strava's own sign-in page - TerritoRide never sees or stores your Strava password.
  • We only ever request read access to your activities. We can't post, edit, or delete anything on your Strava account.
  • From each activity we only read its name, start time, and GPS route - nothing else.
  • Your Strava connection is stored encrypted, not in plain text.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the profile page.
  • You can disconnect at any time from Strava's own "My Apps" settings - we're notified immediately and stop storing your access.
