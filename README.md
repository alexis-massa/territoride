# TerritoRide

A location-based strategy game built on real-world sports activities. Connect
Strava, and every ride, run, or hike becomes a move on a living, competitive
map.

## Concept

The world is made of two systems:

- **Territories** — areas of land captured by riding/running a closed loop
  around them. Can be stolen by a rival who encloses them with a larger or
  better-placed loop.
- **Points of Interest (POIs)** — mountain passes, summits, viewpoints,
  lakes, castles, monuments, landmarks. Captured only by physically passing
  near the location during an activity. Territory ownership never grants a
  POI — you have to go there yourself, and so does anyone who wants to take
  it from you.

The world is dynamic: every territory and POI has a value that drifts over
time. Popular objectives lose value the more they're farmed; neglected ones
quietly become more valuable. The most rewarding targets are often not the
famous ones — they're the forgotten ones. This is meant to push players
toward curiosity and exploration rather than repeating the same local loop
every day.

There's no single winning strategy — some players will hoard territory,
others will specialize in collecting passes and summits, others will become
explorers hunting down neglected high-value spots.

## Vision

A persistent world shaped entirely by the movement of athletes — a blend of
exploration, strategy, cartography, outdoor adventure, and friendly rivalry,
where the map is the game board and real-world movement is the input device.
